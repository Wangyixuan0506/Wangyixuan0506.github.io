Understanding road crash severity

Which factors were associated with fatal or serious injury crashes in New South Wales?

Data and methods

Our team analysed 92,189 crashes recorded by Transport for NSW from 2020 to 2024. The report included exploratory analysis, logistic regression, and random forest modelling.

What we found

Involvement of a vulnerable road user was associated with higher odds of a fatal or serious injury crash in the adjusted logistic model.

Limitations

The two models used different test splits. A comparison of their predictive performance would require evaluating both models on the same held-out crashes.
