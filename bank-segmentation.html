Banking customer segmentation

Can recorded product use and campaign engagement reveal distinct groups of banking customers?

My contribution

I led the clustering workstream. I profiled six source tables, aggregated features at customer level, retained customers with no recorded uptake, and compared K-means and Mean Shift.

Validation

I evaluated separation, group sizes, and resampling stability. The three-cluster K-means model achieved a silhouette score of 0.246 on validation data and 0.248 on the test split.

What I learned

The assignments were stable under resampling, but removing uptake features changed them substantially (adjusted Rand index 0.172). I would compare the model against a simple uptake-based rule before using it for a marketing pilot.
