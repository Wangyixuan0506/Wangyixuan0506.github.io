Research in progress · R

Survival after colon cancer treatment

How much longer did patients in the combined-treatment group survive, on average, within five years of randomisation?

Data

The project uses public trial data from the R survival package. The analysis focuses on overall survival and uses the death endpoint records.

Analysis plan

Plot Kaplan–Meier survival curves with numbers at risk, estimate the five-year restricted mean survival time difference with a 95% confidence interval, and compare its interpretation with a Cox hazard ratio.

Sensitivity analysis

Assess how the restricted mean survival time difference changes with the follow-up horizon, and use Schoenfeld residuals to assess the proportional hazards assumption.
