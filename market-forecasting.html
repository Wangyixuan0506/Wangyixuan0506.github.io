Can macro indicators forecast equity returns?

Can US macroeconomic indicators improve forecasts of next-month equity returns?

My contribution

I led the macroeconomic analysis for our team project. I aligned monthly indicators and index returns, then compared linear regression, random forest, and XGBoost using chronological validation.

Benchmark results

In the primary S&P 500 analysis, the validation-selected random forest had a test RMSE of 4.860 percentage points, compared with 4.843 for the historical-mean benchmark. In the common-period comparison, none of the three models beat its market benchmark on the test set.

Limitations

The backtest used revised macroeconomic data and approximated release delays. A real-time evaluation would need the data vintages and release dates that were available at each forecast date.
