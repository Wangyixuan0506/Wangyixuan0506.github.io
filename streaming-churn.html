Predicting streaming subscriber churn

How should a streaming service prioritise outreach when likely churners make up fewer than one in five records?

Data and methods

I analysed 14,035 course-provided records, of which 17.86% were positive for churn. I excluded direct identifiers and payment fields, compared support vector, decision tree, and random forest classifiers, and used validation data to choose a threshold.

Held-out results

The final support vector classifier achieved 0.400 average precision versus a no-skill reference of about 0.179. It identified 334 of 501 churners, with 66.7% recall and 33.2% precision.

Limitations

The dataset did not include the outcomes of retention offers. A controlled trial would be needed to measure whether a discount changes customer behaviour.
